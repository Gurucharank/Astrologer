About the Astrologer

Astro Guruji

An astrologer is a practitioner of astrology, the ancient art and science of interpreting celestial movements and their influence on human life and events. Using tools like horoscopes, natal charts, and planetary alignments, astrologers provide insights into personality traits, life paths, and potential challenges or opportunities. Astrologers often specialize in various branches of astrology, such as natal (birth) astrology, predictive astrology, relationship compatibility, or vocational guidance. They combine their knowledge of the zodiac signs, planets, houses, and aspects to deliver personalized readings that help individuals understand themselves better and make informed decisions.
